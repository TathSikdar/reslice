# ADR-005 — A gantry-tilted series is rejected, not resampled

## Context

CT gantries can be tilted, typically to angle the scan plane along a structure or away from
the lenses of the eyes. When they are, the slices no longer stack perpendicular to their own
plane: the image plane's normal (`row × column` from `ImageOrientationPatient`) and the
direction the slices actually step in (`IPP₁ − IPP₀`) diverge by the tilt angle, and the
acquired data fills a sheared box rather than a rectangular one.

Everything downstream of the loader assumes a rectangular voxel grid. `Volume` is a flat
`short[]` with an affine transform to patient space; the reslicer walks a plane through it
and samples trilinearly. Hand a tilted series to that machinery unchanged and it produces an
image that looks entirely normal and is wrong by the tilt angle — the geometry is skewed,
so every distance, every ROI area and every anatomical position is off by an amount that
grows with distance from the reference slice.

There are two honest options. Resample the tilted acquisition onto a rectangular grid at
load time, which is what commercial workstations do. Or refuse it.

## Decision

Refuse it. `GeometryValidator` compares the plane normal with the slice-step direction and
throws a `SeriesRejectedException` carrying a typed reason when they diverge beyond about
2.5 degrees. The shell shows the message in the middle of the empty window.

## Consequences

**Tilted studies do not load.** That is a real capability gap and it is stated in the
README's limitations rather than left to be discovered.

**No silently wrong images.** The failure mode this avoids is the one that matters: a
resampling bug does not look like a bug. It produces a plausible image with quietly
incorrect geometry, and the person reading it has no way to tell. A refusal is visible,
narratable, and impossible to mistake for a successful load.

**The correctness argument is the point.** Resampling a tilted volume means choosing an
interpolation kernel, deciding what happens at the edges of the sheared box, and validating
the result against something. This project has no tilted ground truth to validate against —
the tolerance itself has only been exercised against synthetic tilt. Shipping an unvalidated
resample of medical images would be the wrong instinct to demonstrate, whatever it did to
the feature list.

**Reversible.** Nothing else in the system would change if a resampling step were added
later: it would sit between the builder and the volume, and everything downstream would
keep the rectangular grid it already assumes. The check is one place, and it would become
a branch rather than a wall.

**The threshold is a calibration knob, not a constant.** 2.5 degrees is wide enough to
absorb the floating-point noise in cosines written to six decimal places and narrow enough
that any deliberate tilt trips it. It wants tuning against a real tilted series, which this
project has not had.
