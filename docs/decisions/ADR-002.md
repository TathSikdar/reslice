# ADR-002 — The affine transform is the single source of truth for volume geometry

## Context

A `Volume` needs to answer "where in the patient is voxel (i, j, k)?" There are two ways
to hold that information.

The first is to store the pieces: an origin, a per-axis spacing, and three direction
cosines, then compose them on demand. The second is to store one 4x4 affine matrix and
derive everything else from it.

The pieces version is easy to read in a debugger and easy to get wrong. Origin, spacing
and orientation can drift out of agreement with each other, and nothing in the type
system objects. Worse, `Spacing` invites a settable property, and a caller that sets
spacing without touching the direction cosines has produced a volume whose stated
geometry and actual geometry disagree.

The two are also not equivalent in expressive power. A gantry-tilted or obliquely
acquired series has a slice step that is not `spacing * normal`, and only the matrix can
represent that.

## Decision

`Volume` holds `VoxelToPatient` (a `Matrix4x4Affine`) and its inverse. `Spacing` and
`Origin` are read-only derived properties: the column magnitudes and the translation
column respectively, documented as derived.

`VolumeBuilder` builds the third column from the **measured** displacement between the
first two slice positions, not from `spacing * normal`.

## Consequences

- There is exactly one place a geometry bug can live. Sampling, reslicing and
  measurement all route through the same transform, so a coordinate convention error is
  wrong everywhere at once rather than subtly wrong in one view.
- `Spacing` for a tilted volume is the magnitude of a leaning column, which is the
  physically correct answer and not obviously so. It is documented at the property.
- The measured-versus-assumed slice step is untestable on an untilted series, because
  the two are equal by definition. A deliberate mutation swapping one for the other
  survived the entire suite until a test at one degree of tilt (inside the rejection
  tolerance, so it still loads) was added.
- No `IVolumeSampler` interface. There is one implementation, and an interface with one
  implementation is a guess about the future dressed as a design.
